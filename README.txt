PERSONAL PORTFOLIO
==================

Files
-----
index.html
styles.css
script.js
assets/profile.jpg

Getting started
---------------
1. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
2. Replace assets/profile.jpg with your profile picture.
3. Open index.html in a browser.
4. Search for "YOUR" in index.html and replace the placeholders.
5. Replace the social "#" links with your real URLs.
6. Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your email address.

Main customization
------------------
The colour palette is defined at the top of styles.css under :root and
html[data-theme="dark"].

Light palette:
#f0f0e9  warm stone background
#e7e8df  soft sage surface
#181813  near-black text
#66685f  muted text
#6f796b  sage accent
#876149  warm brown accent

Dark palette:
#151511  near-black background
#1d1e19  dark sage surface
#eeeee5  warm light text
#a5a79b  muted text
#a4ad9c  light sage accent
#9b7357  warm brown accent

No framework is required. The site uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
The only external resource is Google Fonts.

Accessibility
-------------
The page includes semantic sections, keyboard-accessible controls,
focus-friendly links, reduced-motion support, readable contrast,
ARIA labels, and responsive touch targets.
